☽ the artist

Hi, I'm Echo Moon Art

Character artist, yarn enthusiast, and professional collector of creative side quests.

I’m an artist and crafter with a particular weakness for fantasy characters, dramatic color palettes, strange little creatures, and projects that inevitably become more complicated than originally planned. Most of my artwork begins with a character, an idea, or a tiny detail I can’t stop thinking about, and grows from there.

I’m especially drawn to the personality behind a design: the expressions, colors, clothing, objects, and tiny choices that make a character feel like they existed long before I started drawing them. When I’m not working digitally, there’s a good chance I have knitting needles, yarn, or some other craft project in my hands. Basically, if it involves making something from nothing, I’m probably interested.
